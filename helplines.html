Supporting others

Worried about a friend?

You don't need the perfect words. Showing up and listening matters most.

1. Ask directly

"I've noticed you seem down lately. How are you really doing?" Asking about suicide does not put the idea in someone's head.

2. Listen without fixing

Let them talk. Avoid "just think positive" — acknowledge that what they feel is real.

3. Help them connect

Offer to sit with them while they call 1771, or help them book a counselling appointment.

4. Look after yourself

Supporting someone is heavy. It's okay to talk to a helpline about how you're feeling too.
