Everyday habits

Quick tips for mental wellness

Tip 1 of 5

🌱 Practise mindfulness

Spend a few minutes each day focusing on your breath or one calming activity. Small, regular moments add up.

Tip 2 of 5

💧 Stay hydrated

Even mild dehydration can affect your energy, focus and mood. Keep a bottle within reach.

Tip 3 of 5

🧘 Take breaks

Step away from screens to stretch or walk outside. A short break often brings back clarity.

Tip 4 of 5

📖 Keep learning

Understanding how your mind works makes it easier to notice when something's off. Start with our guide.

Tip 5 of 5

🤝 Reach out

Talking to friends, family or a professional can lighten the load. See who you can call.
