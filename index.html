Quick Tips for Mental Wellness

🌱 Practice Mindfulness: Spend a few minutes each day focusing on your breath or a calming activity.

💧 Stay Hydrated: Drinking enough water can boost your energy and improve your mood.

🧘 Take Breaks: Step away from screens and stretch or walk outside for mental clarity.

📖 Learn Continuously: Explore mental health resources and stay informed.

🤝 Reach Out: Talking to friends, family, or professionals can provide support when you need it.
